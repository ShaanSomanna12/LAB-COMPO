# Laboratory Component Management Platform

## Overview
The Laboratory Component Management Platform is a web-based enterprise-grade application designed to automate and streamline the administration of physical laboratory hardware within academic and research institutions (specifically tailored for Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering). 

It resolves systemic operational inefficiencies related to students borrowing electronic components, tools, and specialized hardware by establishing a rigorous digital chain of custody.

## Key Features

- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Distinct portals and capabilities for Students, Faculty/Lab Admins, Department HODs, and Super Admins.
- **Digital Chain of Custody:** Enforces accountability by authenticating users via their University Serial Number (USN) and requiring photographic evidence at checkout and return.
- **Automated Workflows:** Dynamically generates formalized requisition letters and No Dues Certificates, complete with digital signatures and undertaking agreements.
- **Real-Time Inventory Management:** Provides live availability, condition tracking, and stock quantities across multiple departments (e.g., ECE, MECH, EEE, CIVIL, EDL).
- **Background Jobs & Notifications:** Automated overdue escalations, direct email routing to HODs/Faculty, and real-time status updates.

## Problem Solved
Traditional management paradigms relying on manual ledgers suffer from data entry errors and lack verifiable accountability, leading to high rates of asset attrition. This platform digitizes the entire lifecycle, ensuring hardware is tracked, verified, and replaced or penalized if lost or damaged.
